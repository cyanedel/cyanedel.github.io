Organizer

Organizer is a media vault that doubles as a manga reader. The project started because I don't like seeing images from my gallery when I scroll through social media (Meta often does this to encourage users to upload content). Another problem I had was that manga pages I'd saved on my phone were showing up in the media picker. I couldn't find an app that could do both, so I built one.

It's available on the Play Store in two editions, one paid and one free.
